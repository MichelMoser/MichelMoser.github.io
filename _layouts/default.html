About me

Bioinformatician at Labor Spiez (Swiss Federal Office for Civil Protection), working where mass spectrometry, high-performance computing and AI meet.

I’m building a proteomics capability around de novo peptide sequencing on a Bruker timsTOF — from LC-MS/MS sample preparation through DIA-NN analysis to validated, reproducible pipelines. I also run the institute’s HPC cluster (Ansible-based automation, storage and virtualisation) and help shape how the lab uses AI and computational methods, including internal LLM strategies.

Background: PhD in Bioinformatics (University of Bern), with roots in genomics and long-read sequencing. I enjoy turning complex data and infrastructure into tools colleagues can actually use — and helping teams build these skills.
